## ✨ Features That Are Out of This World!

* 🤖 **AI Hand Gesture Controls (NEW):** Integrated with Google MediaPipe Tasks Vision. Use your webcam to control the universe with your hands!
    * **Pinch to Zoom:** Bring your index finger and thumb close together to zoom in, and pull them apart to zoom out.
    * **Hand Orbit:** Move your open hand in front of the camera to smoothly rotate and tilt the 3D perspective of the solar system.
* 🎬 **Cinematic Intro:** Begins with an anime-style video preloader that can be easily skipped after 5 seconds.
* ☄️ **Meteor Shower:** Following the intro, a fiery meteor shower effect shakes the screen, seamlessly transitioning into the beautiful solar system view.
* 🌌 **Live Universe Background:** A dynamic, ever-moving cosmos filled with colorful nebulae and animated stars.
* 🖱️ **Interactive 3D & 2D Mouse Controls:**
    * **Vertical Drag:** Drag up and down to tilt the solar system in a **3D perspective**.
    * **Horizontal Drag:** Drag left and right to rotate the system **360-degrees in 2D**.
* ➕➖ **Zoom Controls:** Use the UI buttons to precisely zoom in and out of the solar system.
* 🚀 **Dynamic Speedometer:** Control the orbital speed of the planets using the slider and view the live multiplier (e.g., 1.5x). The slider dynamically shifts from **green to red** as speed increases.
* 🌟 **Magical Star Cursor:** Your mouse cursor transforms into a glowing star that leaves a colorful trail behind it.
* 🪐 **Planet & Sun Details:** Click on the Sun or any planet to reveal a beautiful **Glassmorphism card** containing planetary statistics, interesting facts, and a direct Wikipedia link.
* 💡 **Dynamic Lighting:** Realistic solar illumination is cast upon the planets, simulating realistic shadows and enhancing the 3D aesthetic.

## 🔧 How to Launch Your Own Solar System

1.  **Clone the repository:**
    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/HR-894/SolarSystem.git
    ```
2.  **Navigate to the project directory:**
    ```bash
    cd SolarSystem
    ```
3.  **Start a local server:**
    *(Note: A local server is highly recommended to prevent browser CORS issues with the MediaPipe AI modules.)*
    ```bash
    npx http-server
    
    # OR using Python
    python3 -m http.server
    ```
4.  **Open your browser:**
    Navigate to `http://localhost:8080` (or the port specified by your server) and grant webcam access.

    ...And watch the magic happen! 🔮
